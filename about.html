About iAdMe

iAdMe is a locality-first short video social platform built to help people discover nearby creators, businesses, communities, and local experiences through short-form video content.

The platform combines hyperlocal discovery, wallet-based engagement, creator unlocks, premium content access, and ScrollScore-powered engagement intelligence.

Our Purpose

iAdMe is designed to make local discovery more engaging, measurable, and creator-friendly. Users can explore short videos based on locality, interact with creators, unlock premium content, and participate in a digital engagement ecosystem.

ScrollScore

ScrollScore is iAdMe's engagement intelligence signal. It is designed to help represent content quality, watch behavior, and audience interaction as a visible platform signal.
