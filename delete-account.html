Account Deletion

Delete Your iAdMe Account

iAdMe allows users to request deletion of their account and associated account data. Account deletion is available directly inside the iAdMe mobile app.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the iAdMe app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Verify your identity using the email OTP sent to your registered email address.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

What Data Is Deleted or Anonymized

When your account deletion request is completed, iAdMe deletes or anonymizes account-related data associated with your profile, including:

  • Profile information
  • Uploaded content associated with your account
  • Comments and social interactions associated with your account
  • Messages associated with your account
  • Notification preferences
  • Account authentication records

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, abuse prevention, or financial record-keeping purposes. Any retained information is handled according to our Privacy Policy.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are typically processed immediately after successful OTP verification. In some cases, complete removal from backups or logs may take additional time as part of normal system retention and security processes.

Need Help?

If you cannot access the app or experience any issue deleting your account, contact us at support@iadme.app from your registered email address.
