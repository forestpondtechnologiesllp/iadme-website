Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 15, 2026

iAdMe is owned and operated by FORESTPOND TECHNOLOGIES LLP.

This Privacy Policy explains how iAdMe collects, uses, stores, shares, protects, and deletes information when users access or use the iAdMe mobile application, website, platform features, wallet services, content services, advertising services, and related services.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use iAdMe, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Name, profile information, profile photo, bio, and public account details
  • Email address, phone number, authentication details, and OTP verification records
  • Uploaded videos, captions, thumbnails, media files, comments, reactions, shares, reports, and other user-generated content
  • Messages and communication data exchanged through iAdMe messaging features
  • Approximate or precise location information, with user permission, for locality-based discovery, feed ranking, nearby content, safety, and platform functionality
  • Wallet, premium unlock, coupon, reward, transaction, and payment-related records
  • Device information, app version, operating system, identifiers, session data, logs, diagnostics, crash reports, and usage analytics
  • Advertising interaction data such as ad impressions, clicks, views, and campaign performance signals
  • Support requests, moderation records, user reports, and communications with iAdMe

How We Use Information

We use information to operate, secure, personalize, and improve iAdMe, including to:

  • Create, verify, authenticate, and protect user accounts
  • Provide short video feeds, locality-first discovery, upload, playback, comments, reactions, messaging, notifications, and social features
  • Improve feed relevance, ranking, recommendations, analytics, and user experience
  • Process wallet activity, premium unlocks, coupons, rewards, and payment-related functionality
  • Send transactional, security, service, moderation, engagement, and support-related notifications
  • Display advertisements or sponsored content and measure advertising performance
  • Detect, prevent, investigate, and respond to fraud, abuse, spam, harmful activity, policy violations, and security incidents
  • Review reports, moderate content, enforce platform rules, and comply with applicable legal obligations
  • Provide customer support and respond to user requests

Location Information

iAdMe is a locality-first short video platform. With user permission, we may collect approximate or precise location information to provide nearby feeds, city, state, national, and international content discovery, location-aware ranking, upload metadata, safety controls, and other location-based platform features.

Users may control location permissions through their device settings. Some locality-based features may not work correctly if location access is disabled.

User Content

Videos, captions, comments, reactions, shares, profile information, and other content submitted by users may be stored, processed, displayed, distributed, moderated, or removed as required to operate the platform, enforce safety standards, respond to reports, and comply with applicable law.

Messages

Messages exchanged through iAdMe may be stored and processed to provide messaging functionality, support delivery, prevent abuse, investigate reports, enforce platform rules, and comply with legal obligations.

Notifications

iAdMe may send push notifications, in-app notifications, SMS, email, or other service communications related to authentication, security, account activity, comments, reactions, messages, premium unlocks, moderation, support, product updates, and platform engagement.

Users may manage notification permissions through app settings or device settings where supported.

Wallet & Payment Information

Wallet recharge, premium unlocks, creator interactions, coupons, rewards, and payment-related services may be processed using third-party payment service providers.

iAdMe does not store full payment card details on its own servers. We may store transaction identifiers, wallet records, unlock records, invoice or payment status information, refund-related records, and other information needed for accounting, fraud prevention, support, compliance, and dispute resolution.

Advertisements and Analytics

iAdMe may display advertisements and sponsored content. We may use advertising-related analytics, such as impressions, clicks, views, engagement, and campaign performance data, to measure campaign effectiveness, improve ad delivery, prevent fraud, and support advertiser reporting.

Content Moderation and Safety

Uploaded content, comments, profiles, messages, reports, and platform activity may be reviewed, moderated, restricted, removed, or retained where necessary to enforce platform policies, investigate abuse, respond to user reports, protect users, prevent fraud, or comply with legal requirements.

Third-Party Services

iAdMe may use third-party infrastructure, hosting, cloud storage, media processing, content delivery, analytics, authentication, communication, notification, crash reporting, payment, advertising, and security service providers. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide services to iAdMe or as otherwise permitted by law.

Data Protection

We implement commercially reasonable technical, administrative, and operational safeguards to help protect user information and platform security. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide iAdMe services, maintain account records, support wallet and payment functionality, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent fraud, investigate abuse, meet security requirements, and comply with legal, accounting, tax, regulatory, or operational obligations.

Some information may be deleted, anonymized, or retained in limited form after account deletion where retention is required or permitted for legal, financial, security, fraud-prevention, moderation, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Account Access, Updates, and Deletion

Users may access or update certain account and profile information through the iAdMe application.

Users may delete their account directly from within the iAdMe application. After an account deletion request, account information may be deleted, deactivated, anonymized, or retained only where necessary for legal, financial, fraud-prevention, security, moderation, dispute-resolution, or regulatory purposes.

For account, privacy, or deletion-related help, users may also contact support at support@iadme.app.

Children

iAdMe is not intended for children under 13 years of age. Users under the age of legal majority in their jurisdiction should use iAdMe only with appropriate parental or guardian consent where required by law.

User Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, users may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of certain personal information by using available in-app controls or by contacting iAdMe support. Some requests may be limited where information is required for security, fraud prevention, financial records, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or platform safety.
