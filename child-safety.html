Safety Standards

iAdMe Child Safety Standards

FORESTPOND TECHNOLOGIES LLP is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation on iAdMe.

Prohibited Content and Conduct

iAdMe strictly prohibits any content, conduct, account activity, or communication involving:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)
  • Grooming, solicitation, or predatory behaviour involving minors
  • Sexualisation of minors
  • Human trafficking or exploitation involving minors
  • Any content or behaviour that exploits, harms, endangers, or abuses children

User Reporting

Users can report content or accounts directly inside the iAdMe app. Reports may include videos, profiles, comments, messages, or other activity that appears to violate our safety standards or platform rules.

Users may also contact us directly at support@iadme.app for urgent child safety concerns.

Review and Enforcement

Child safety reports are reviewed by our moderation team. Depending on the severity and nature of the violation, iAdMe may take action including:

  • Removing violating content
  • Restricting account functionality
  • Suspending or permanently terminating accounts
  • Preserving relevant information where required for safety, security, or legal purposes
  • Reporting to relevant regional or national authorities where required by applicable law

Cooperation With Authorities

iAdMe complies with applicable child safety laws and cooperates with valid legal requests from relevant authorities in connection with child safety investigations, child sexual abuse material, or child exploitation concerns.

Ongoing Safety Commitment

We continue to improve our reporting, moderation, and enforcement processes to help keep iAdMe safe for users and to prevent misuse of the platform.
